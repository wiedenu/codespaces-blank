## Key points (as published)

- Rep. Ashley Hinson's campaign spent nearly $110,000 on luxury hotels and fine dining.
- Expenses included upscale DC restaurants, private clubs, and exclusive resorts across the US.
- Hinson publicly described herself as a "working mom" familiar with financial struggles.
